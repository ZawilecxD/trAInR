# Product

## Register

product

## Users

trAInR serves independent personal trainers and their clients. Trainers plan at a desk or tablet while managing a small roster, building exercises, composing sessions, assigning workouts, and reviewing logged performance. Clients use a phone in portrait during a real gym session, often one-handed, to see what to do next and record sets without losing focus.

## Product Purpose

trAInR replaces the spreadsheet, chat, and PDF loop used by independent trainers with one async coaching workflow: exercise library -> session template -> assigned calendar session -> guided client logging -> trainer review. Success means trainers can assign useful sessions quickly, clients can log actual workout data with confidence, and the trainer-client feedback loop is visible end to end.

## Brand Personality

Focused, athletic, and precise. The interface should feel like a trustworthy training instrument: calm enough for trainer planning, energetic enough for active gym use, and never noisy for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Do not make the app look like a generic SaaS dashboard, a neon gamer fitness app, a spreadsheet clone, or a lifestyle wellness feed. Avoid template AI patterns such as hero metric blocks, repeated icon-card grids, gradient text, decorative glassmorphism, and visual flourishes that compete with workout data.

## Design Principles

1. Design for the phone-at-the-gym moment first; primary client actions must be reachable, glanceable, and tolerant of distraction.
2. Keep trainer authoring calm and structured; dense screens should help the trainer move faster without hiding state.
3. Treat workout data as precious; saving, errors, completion state, and editability must always be explicit.
4. Preserve role clarity; clients should never see trainer-only controls, and trainers should quickly understand which client needs attention.
5. Make the athletic identity specific to trAInR's async coaching loop, not to generic fitness imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and visible keyboard focus. Maintain 44px minimum touch targets for primary workout logging controls, support reduced motion for any animation, avoid color-only status communication, and keep client logging copy plain enough to read mid-workout.
