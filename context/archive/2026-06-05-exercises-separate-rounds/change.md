## Notes

S-14 from `context/foundation/roadmap.md`.

Trainer should be able to specify each round of the exercise with separate data (load, rest, reps etc). For example trainer selects "Benchpress" and easily sets 1st round to 10x50kg and 2min rest, 2nd round 8x60kg and 2min rest, 3rd round to 6x70kg and 3 min rest
